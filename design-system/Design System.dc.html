Foundations · 03

Motion

One easing curve — cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1) — unifies reveal, lift, and glide. All motion honors prefers-reduced-motion.

Shared easing — cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1) on every transition
One easing curve unifies all motion. Scroll entrances rise 18px and fade over 0.8s, staggered 90ms between siblings. Hovers lift 1px (buttons), 4px (cards), 5px (plates). Arrows glide 4px right. Everything disables under prefers-reduced-motion.
