12 Books to Read With Your Middle Schooler

A free printable reading list — so you can stay weird and wonderful together.

Twelve books worth reading alongside your kid in the strange, tender middle-school years — each with a summary, why it matters, and read-along notes for the conversations the books will start.

You'll also get Fletchling Thoughts — cozy letters on books, home, and making meaning, every other week. Unsubscribe anytime.

What's inside

Fourteen pages. Twelve books. One very good year of reading.

Book one
The Song of the Lioness

A girl trades places with her twin and trains as a knight in disguise. Rachel's single most re-read series — and a reminder of what fiercely pursuing your purpose looks like.

Book two
The Little House Series

Time for the middle-school reread. Candle making, cast-iron cooking, fiddle songs — aspirational literature that makes chores feel kinda cool.

Book three
The Secret Garden

An unwanted orphan, a walled garden, and a family brought back to life — for the year the hormones make your kid feel like nobody loves them.

…and nine more, each with a summary, why it matters, and honest notes on the mature moments worth talking through together.

From one reader to another

I'm Rachel — a mother of four, a knitter, a farmhouse cook, and a writer of folkloric fiction from the Shenandoah Valley. These are the books my sisters and I read by candlelight as tweens, and the ones I'm now reading with my own kids. They're the list I wish someone had handed me.
